# The 27 Nakshatras (Lunar Mansions)

Each nakshatra spans 13°20′ of the sidereal zodiac (360° ÷ 27). Each is divided
into 4 **padas** of 3°20′. The Vimshottari dasha lord of a nakshatra repeats every
9 mansions (Ketu, Venus, Sun, Moon, Mars, Rahu, Jupiter, Saturn, Mercury).

## Padas

Each pada is 3°20′. Padas map to navamsa (D9) signs and are used in finer chart
work. The skill reports the pada (1–4) per planet; full navamsa charting is out of
scope for v1.

> Themes above are traditional cultural associations, not predictions.
